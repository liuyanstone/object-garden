Familiar things, gentle illustrations

当前图库精选 300 种常见物品，统一采用圆润线条、简洁平面造型与莫兰迪色系。每局随机出现 150 种不同物品。所有图片都由本站提供，游戏过程中无需访问外部图片网站。

  • 269 illustrations adapted from OpenMoji, by the OpenMoji contributors listed in the individual manifest. CC BY-SA 4.0. Adaptations: unified muted colors, rounded 2-unit outlines, consistent 72-unit canvas, everyday English/Chinese labels. These adapted SVGs remain CC BY-SA 4.0.
  • 31 original SVG illustrations created for this game, including the kettle, rice cooker, vacuum cleaner and other familiar objects. These use the same color palette, line weight and flat illustration language.
  • Names have been curated and simplified for recognition. Some base names come from Unicode CLDR: Unicode License.
